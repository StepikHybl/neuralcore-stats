NeuralCore Stats

A small private tool of the YouTube channel NeuralCore.

NeuralCore Stats reads the statistics of the NeuralCore YouTube channel (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) for its owner, so the channel's own analytics can be reviewed in one place on the owner's computer.

  • Read-only access: it never uploads, edits or deletes anything.
  • Only the channel owner signs in; it is not offered to anyone else.
  • The data stays on the owner's computer and is not shared with third parties.
